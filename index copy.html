About Rise Care Foundation

Dedicated to building a stronger, more equitable future

Founded with a deep commitment to human dignity, Rise Care Foundation bridges the gap between underserved communities and sustainable opportunity. We empower through education, vocational training, healthcare access, and compassionate community outreach.

🎯 Mission First

Empowering marginalized groups with vital resources and mentorship.

🌟 Complete Transparency

100% of public donations directly fund grassroots programs.

EST. 2012

Empowering Communities

Every child deserves quality education; every mother deserves economic independence and support.

What We Do

Our Core Programs

Targeted interventions designed to create long-lasting, generational change across multiple pillars of society.

🎓 Education & Scholarships

Empowering minds through accessible learning tools.

Providing tuition assistance, books, digital tools, and safe learning spaces for brilliant children in need, ensuring a 98% graduation rate.

👩‍🦰 Women's Empowerment

Building economic independence and community resilience.

Equipping women with micro-grants, vocational skills training, financial literacy, and entrepreneurship mentorship to start sustainable businesses.

🍲 Health & Nutrition

Nurturing bodies and bringing healthcare to rural zones.

Distributing wholesome meals, ensuring clean drinking water access, and organizing mobile medical clinics in remote villages in need.

🤝 Youth Leadership

Cultivating the next generation of ethical changemakers.

Mentorship programs, sports leagues, and civic engagement workshops fostering leadership and confidence in young adults.
