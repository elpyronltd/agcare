Our Mission & Vision

Transforming Lives with Hope, Dignity & Sustainable Action

At Rise Care Foundation, we believe every individual deserves the opportunity to thrive regardless of their circumstances. We are a non-profit organization dedicated to breaking cycles of poverty through holistic community support, quality education, healthcare, and women’s economic empowerment.

Charity & Support

Direct essential relief, clean water, and emergency aid.

Youth Education

Scholarships, digital labs, and teacher support.

Women Leadership

Micro-business grants, vocational skills, and mentorship.

Community Building

Sustainable local infrastructure and health clinics.
