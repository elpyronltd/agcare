About Us

Hope grows fastest when a whole community is lifted together.

Rise Care Foundation is a non-profit that works alongside families, schools and local leaders. We meet urgent needs today and build the skills and opportunities that last, with a special focus on girls and women.

Charity

Relief and care for households in need.

Community support

Food, health and safe spaces that neighbours share.

Education

Fees, kits and mentors that keep children learning.

Women empowerment

Skills, savings and leadership for lasting independence.
